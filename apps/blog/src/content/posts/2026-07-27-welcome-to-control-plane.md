I spent 16+ years as a software engineer. I'm now pivoting into IT Audit and
technology assurance — starting with ISACA's IT Audit Fundamentals
certificate, then CISA.

**Control Plane** is where I'll document that transition: what I'm learning,
what surprises me, and eventually what the work actually looks like once I'm
doing it professionally.

The name is deliberate. A "control plane," in engineering terms, is the part
of a system that governs everything else. In audit, a "control" is the thing
that's supposed to keep a system correct, secure, or compliant. Every post
here starts from the same question: **what's the engineering reality behind
this control?** Not just what the framework says should happen, but what's
actually happening inside the pipelines, the access models, the
infrastructure — and where those two pictures agree or diverge.

That question doesn't box this blog into information security audit, or
financial-systems IT audit, or any one specialty. It applies wherever there's
a system and a control watching it — which, in 2026, is everywhere in IT.
