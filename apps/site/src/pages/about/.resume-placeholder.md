Do not add a `resume/` route here in Astro.

The resume is served from its own subdomain, resume.oruganti.dev, as a
separate Cloudflare Pages project (see terraform/main.tf and
.github/workflows/deploy-resume.yml) fed directly by the separate `resume`
repo's `resume-dist` build artifact — this app never builds or bundles it.

`/about/resume` on this site is just a static 301 redirect to
resume.oruganti.dev, defined in apps/site/public/_redirects. Adding an
Astro page/route at this path would conflict with that redirect.
